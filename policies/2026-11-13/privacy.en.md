# Privacy Policy — 6529 Validator Monitor

*Last updated: 13 November 2026*

This policy covers 6529 Validator Monitor for iPhone and Apple Watch, and for Android phones and Wear OS watches. In short: no account, no wallet connection, no ads, no tracking. The app reads public network data, and what you follow stays on your devices.

## No account, no wallet, no tracking

There is no account to create and nothing to sign. The app never connects to a wallet and never asks for a signature or a transaction. It shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics software, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## What the app reads

To show a validator's day, the app downloads public network data: the 6529-network-data files that Casa NUA publishes on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com; versions released before 12 November 2026 read the same kind of files from 6529scan, also on GitHub), 6529's list of validator releases (6529.io), and, when you type a 6529 handle or address, that account's public profile from 6529 (api.6529.io). These requests carry what you typed or public addresses, and, as with any website, those services see your device's network address. Each service has its own privacy policy. From versions released after 13 November 2026, the app also downloads its descriptive texts (explanations, glossary and similar wording, so they can be corrected without an app update) from Casa NUA's own server (nua-published-curations.casanua.workers.dev, on Cloudflare). That request carries nothing about you or the accounts you follow; as with any website, the server sees your device's network address, and it keeps no request log.

## What stays on your devices

The account you follow, any other accounts you add, the last day downloaded and a log of the days this device has seen are kept on your phone, shared only with the app's own widgets and with your own watch: your paired Apple Watch, or on Android your Wear OS watch, which receives through Wear OS, Google's link between your phone and your watch, the account you follow and whether you are subscribed. We cannot see them. Deleting the app from your phone and your watch deletes them.

## Alerts

The app's reminders and alerts are made on your phone by the app itself, from the public data above; nothing is sent to us.

On Android there are no instant alerts, so nothing below applies there. On iPhone, instant alerts are optional and off until you turn them on. When on, our alert service (on Cloudflare) keeps exactly this: your iPhone's notification token (an address Apple creates for this install), the public wallet of the validator account you follow, which alert switches are on, and which of today's alerts your iPhone already showed. No name, handle, e-mail or network address is kept, and the service keeps no request log. Turning instant alerts off deletes the entry at once; an entry your iPhone has not renewed for 7 days is deleted by itself, and so is one Apple reports as no longer valid. Because instant alerts are part of the subscription, an entry also shows that the app on that iPhone is subscribed.

## Subscription

On iPhone, the subscription is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether your Apple Account holds the subscription, and passes the answer only to your own Apple Watch. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account's subscription settings.

On Android, the subscription is sold by Google through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play on your phone whether your Google Account holds the subscription, and passes the answer only to your own Wear OS watch. You can manage or cancel it in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions.

## Siri, widgets and watches

Siri answers, widgets, the watch apps and, on Android, the Status shortcut (which speaks its answer with your phone's own text-to-speech) read the same public data and the same on-device information described above. Nothing about them is sent to us.

## What the app does not use

The app does not use your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

If this policy changes, this page changes with it and the date above is updated.
