# Support — 6529 Validator Monitor

Help with 6529 Validator Monitor on iPhone and Apple Watch. Android and Wear OS are in testing.

## Write to us

Questions, problems or ideas: write to 6529monitor@nua.casa.

It helps if you say which phone and watch you use, the app's version, and the handle you follow (it is public). Never send us a private key or a recovery phrase; we will never ask for one.

We keep your e-mail only as long as needed to answer you, and we delete it when you ask.

## The data looks old

The app shows what one 6529 validator node, run by Casa NUA, sees. Its collector publishes every 10 to 60 minutes, so what you see can be up to about an hour behind the network.

To refresh, pull down on the main screen, or open the ••• menu at the top and choose “Refresh”. Widgets and watch faces are refreshed by the system on its own schedule and can lag behind the app.

When the data on screen was published more than two hours ago, the app shows this line above the day, with or without the subscription: “Last update …. The source may have stopped publishing.” With the subscription you can also switch on the “Source stopped” alert. When this happens, nothing is wrong with your validator or your phone: the app simply has no fresh data. Check your own node, and write to us if it lasts.

The collector signs the files. The app checks the signature before showing the data from iPhone version 1.0 (build 8) and Android version 1.0 (4) onward; earlier test builds did not. A file that fails the check is not shown, and the app keeps the last good day.

## My validator shows as not signed, but I think it signed

The app shows one node's view of the network. A signature can take a while to reach that node and to be published, so the app can still say “Not signed yet today.” when your validator has already signed.

Wait for the next update and refresh. For anything that matters, check your own validator node: it is the authority, not this app. Also check that the handle or wallet you follow in the app is the one your validator signs with.

If the app still disagrees with your node after about an hour, write to 6529monitor@nua.casa with the handle and the day. Do not rely on this app as your only check that your validator signed.

## What is free and what the subscription adds

Free: the day (your signature, the verdict and the ring), the network map and the glossary, for one account.

On iPhone, the subscription adds: widgets on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen; the Apple Watch app and its complications; alerts; Siri and Shortcuts; up to 5 accounts in all; history, streak and countdown to the close.

On Android, it adds: widgets on the home screen; the Wear OS app and its complications; alerts; the “Status” shortcut, which says the day aloud; up to 5 accounts in all; history, streak and countdown to the close. There is no Lock Screen widget on Android.

It is a monthly subscription that renews until you cancel. The price in your country is shown by the App Store or Google Play before you subscribe (for example about US$2 a month in the United States and R$5 a month in Brazil).

While you use the TestFlight beta, Apple does not charge for the subscription, and it does not carry over to the App Store version.

## Cancel or get a refund

The subscription is sold and billed by Apple or Google, so cancelling and refunds happen there.

1. iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose the app's subscription and cancel it. Or, in the app: the ••• menu, “Subscription”, then “Manage subscription”.
2. Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose the app's subscription and cancel it. Or, in the app: the ••• menu, “Subscription”, then “Manage subscription”.

After you cancel, the subscription stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Refunds: on iPhone, ask Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, ask in Google Play, in your order history. Apple and Google decide refunds under their own policies; we cannot issue one ourselves.

In Brazil you may withdraw from the purchase within 7 days and get your money back (Consumer Defence Code, art. 49). The refund is requested from Apple or Google, as above. Write to 6529monitor@nua.casa and we will help. Your rights under consumer law are not affected by anything on this page.

## Restore purchases

If you reinstalled the app or changed phones, open the ••• menu, choose “Subscribe” and tap “Restore purchases”. You must be signed in to the same Apple Account or Google Account that bought the subscription.

On Android the app also asks Google Play by itself every time it opens, so the subscription normally comes back without tapping anything.

A subscription bought on iPhone does not carry over to Android, or the other way round: each store sells its own.

## Widgets on iPhone

Widgets are part of the subscription. In the widget list the app appears as “6529 Validator”. On the Home Screen it comes in two sizes, small and large. On the Lock Screen it comes as a circle, a rectangle and a line of text.

## Apple Watch

1. Install 6529 Validator Monitor on your iPhone, add your account there and subscribe there. The account cannot be typed on the watch: the iPhone hands it over.
2. Install the app on the watch. If it is not there yet, open the Watch app on the iPhone and install “6529 Validator” from the list of available apps.
3. Open the app on the watch. If it says “Open the app on your iPhone to choose the account to follow.”, open the app on the iPhone; the watch receives the account the next time it wakes.
4. To put the ring on your watch face: touch and hold the watch face, tap Edit, choose a complication slot and pick “6529 Validator”.

The Watch app and its complications are part of the subscription. Without it, the watch says “The Watch app is part of the subscription. Subscribe in the iPhone app.”

Once it has your account, the watch downloads the public data by itself, so it stays current when the iPhone is asleep or far away.

## Alerts do not arrive

Alerts are part of the subscription. They are made on your phone by the app; no server sends them.

The switches are in the app: open the ••• menu and choose “Alerts”. There is one switch per alert: “Not signed yet”, “Signed”, “Day agreed”, “Signed another result”, “Day not signed”, “New version” and “Source stopped”.

If that screen says “Notifications are off for this app.”, tap “Open Settings” and allow notifications. Check also that a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is not hiding them.

The “Not signed yet” reminders are set ahead of time and normally arrive on time (on Android, sometimes a few minutes late). The other alerts arrive when the app next reads the network, and the phone's system decides when the app may do that in the background, so an alert can come later than the event, or not at all if the phone was offline. On Android, battery-saving settings can delay them further.
