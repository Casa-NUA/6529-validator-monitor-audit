## 2. What the app is, and what it is not

2.1. **Read-only.** You type a public 6529 handle or wallet address and the app shows whether that account's validator signed today, with related public network information. There is no login. The app never connects to a wallet, never asks for keys or a recovery phrase, and signs nothing. Never type a private key or a recovery phrase into it.

2.2. **Independent.** The app is made by Casa NUA. **It is not made by, affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by 6529.** “6529” and related names belong to their owners and are used only to describe what the app reads.

2.3. **Unofficial data from one node.** The network data comes from a single 6529 validator node run by Casa NUA and is published in a public repository on GitHub. The collector signs the files. The app checks the signature before showing the data from iPhone version 1.0 (build 8) and Android version 1.0 (4) onward; earlier test builds did not. The signature shows that a file came from Casa NUA's collector and was not altered on the way; it does not show that what the node saw is right. It is one node's view, not an official 6529 publication. **It can be late (normally up to about 10 minutes; longer if the source stops), incomplete or wrong.** Validator locations are declared by the validators themselves and are not verified.

2.4. **Do not rely on the app as your only check that your validator signed.** Alerts are made on your device and depend on your device, its settings and its connection; they can arrive late or not at all. Check your own validator node for anything that matters.

2.5. **No financial advice.** The app shows no token prices or market data, and gives no financial, investment, legal or tax advice.
