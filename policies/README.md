# Privacy policy, terms and support text: every published version

Each dated folder holds the texts as they were published on that date for the 6529 Validator Monitor app,
in English and Portuguese. Nothing here is edited after publication; a new version gets a new folder.
The current texts are always at:

- Privacy: https://app.nua.casa/?app=monitor#privacy
- Terms: https://app.nua.casa/?app=monitor#terms
- Support: https://app.nua.casa/?app=monitor#support

| Folder | What changed |
|---|---|
| `2026-11-13/` | The first privacy policy published for the app. There were no terms or support text written for it yet. |
| `2026-11-14/` | After audit no. 1 (findings M3 and M4): the privacy policy names the publisher, lists what the app sends and to whom, users' rights and a contact; Terms of Use and a support page written for this app. They say from which app versions the signature check and some Android protections apply. |
| `2026-11-14b/` | Support text only: the collector now publishes every 5 to 60 minutes (it was 10 to 60). The privacy policy and terms are unchanged. |
| `2026-11-15/` | Support text and Terms (clause 2.3) only: the collector now publishes every 5 minutes, so the data is normally up to about 10 minutes behind the network (the texts said up to about an hour). The privacy policy is unchanged. |
| `2026-11-16/` | Privacy policy only: a new item 5, the live chain server (monitor6529-live.casanua.workers.dev), which the app reads only while its Chain page is open; the old item 5 becomes 6. Terms and support are unchanged. |
| `2026-11-16b/` | Privacy policy only: item 5 now also covers the main screen (the app reads the live chain server when you open or refresh it, and every few seconds while the Chain page is open). |
| `2026-11-16c/` | Privacy policy only: instant alerts on iPhone (optional, off by default) are described before the server that sends them is switched on: what the server keeps while the option is on, the Apple DeviceCheck proof, the brief IP use, deletion, and the short-lived "alert already sent" marks; consent as their legal basis; the retention and rights sections name this one exception. Terms and support are unchanged. |
| `2026-11-16d/` | Privacy policy only: seven sentences that still said nothing reaches Casa NUA (the summary, the list of what the app sends, the alerts, what stays on your devices, consent, your rights, children) now carry the same instant-alerts exception; the list of what the app sends gains item 7, the alert server, used only if you switch instant alerts on. Terms and support are unchanged. |
| `2026-11-18/` | Privacy policy only: instant alerts on iPhone are now on by default for Pro subscribers and can be switched off; the legal basis changes from consent to performance of the contract (LGPD art. 7, V; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). What the server keeps is unchanged. Terms and support are unchanged. |
